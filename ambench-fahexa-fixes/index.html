FA-Hexa Task Fixes

Left: the original AM-Bench code (official scripted expert, official success check). Right: after the fixes. All clips play together on a loop. Clips have different lengths, so use “Restart all” to line their starts up again. The overlay shows simulation time, and a green SUCCESS appears once the task's success check fires.

Each clip runs until success plus 5 s, or for the full episode if the task never succeeds.

Toss Ball

TossBall-Am-FAHexa-Abs-PID-Direct-v0

What was wrong

  • At reset the fingers were closed, so the 8 cm ball was spawned overlapping them and the physics engine forced them apart.
  • The expert starts by pitching the gripper 36° down. FA-Hexa's end effector sits close to its body, so the fingers hit the drone's own rotor arm, get pushed shut, and squeeze the ball out.
  • The expert ignores the container's x position, so even a held ball only lands in the bin by chance.

What changed

  • At reset the fingers open to the ball's width and the ball is seated against the palm (shared fix, see Peg in Hole).
  • New expert TossBallLobPolicy: carries the ball with the gripper level to a run-up point behind the bin and moves forward smoothly.
  • Every step it predicts where the ball would land from the ball's measured position and velocity, and opens the gripper when that prediction reaches the bin center. It keeps its speed until the fingers have fully let go, so the ball isn't dragged back.
  • After release it backs off, so the base is more than 0.7 m behind the bin when the ball lands (part of the official success check). The success check is unchanged.

Wipe Window

WipeWindow-Am-FAHexa-Abs-PID-Direct-v0

What was wrong

  • The expert hard-codes the sponge face at 0.215 m ahead of the end effector. On the way in, the sponge is already touching the glass while the expert still lifts it by 10 cm.
  • Friction from the glass holds the sponge while the gripper moves up, pulling it out of the fingers until it drops.
  • Each stain takes about 7 s, so four stains don't fit in the 20 s episode.

What changed

  • New expert WipeWindowPressPolicy: moves sideways 6 cm off the glass to face each stain, presses straight in along the window normal, holds 0.3 s, and backs straight out.
  • Before each press it re-measures where the sponge actually sits in the hand and aligns and sets the press depth from that.
  • It reads which stains are already gone and skips them; all four are cleared in about 6–10 s. The success check is unchanged.

Frame Assembly

FrameAssembly-Am-FAHexa-Abs-PID-Direct-v0

What was wrong

  • The success check was too loose: the frame center only had to be within 10 cm of the peg midpoint, so a frame hanging on the peg tips, not against the wall, counted.
  • The expert grasps the horizontal top bar with a level gripper whose fingers close along the bar, so it never really holds it; the frame hangs on the fingers and swings.
  • The insertion only goes to about the middle of the pegs, so the frame is never pushed fully on.

What changed

  • New success check: frame within 2 cm of flush against the wall, within 3 cm of the peg center, less than 10° off the wall plane, and at rest for 0.25 s.
  • New expert FrameAssemblyBarGraspPolicy: rolls the gripper 90° and clamps the top bar from above and below near the fingertips; comes in high and drops down behind the frame so it doesn't knock it over.
  • In front of the pegs it measures the frame center and aligns it, then pushes until the frame is flush (tracking the live frame-in-hand offset), lets go, and backs away.
  • For this task only: FA-Hexa finger stiffness 100 → 1000 (the stock grip on the 2 cm bar was under 1 N), and the base x limit relaxed from 1.0 to 1.25 m (otherwise the arm can't reach the wall).

Open Door

OpenDoor-Am-FAHexa-Abs-PID-Direct-v0

What was wrong

  • The success check only asked for the door to open past 20°, and the expert simply pushed the door leaf open with a closed gripper, never touching the handle.

What changed

  • New success check: the hinge is latched at reset and only unlocks after the gripper has closed at the handle for 0.2 s. Success = unlatched and opened past 20°.
  • New expert OpenDoorHandlePolicy: yaws the gripper to the door and rolls it 90°, aligns closed-loop 10 cm in front of the handle (error under 8 mm), then moves straight in along the door normal and closes.
  • Once unlatched, it swings the door along the handle's arc about the hinge to about 30°, lets go, and backs away.

Limitation: in the door asset the lever sits flush with the door face and has no collision shape, so it can't physically be pinched. Here “operating the handle” means closing the gripper at the handle to release the latch, not truly gripping the lever.

Peg in Hole

PegInHole-Am-FAHexa-Abs-PID-Direct-v0

What was wrong

  • Unreliable with the original code (6 of 8 runs). The peg is held and brought to the hole, but the expert only advances the peg tip to the hole plane while the check requires it to pass that plane, so there is zero margin and it often stops just short.
  • It shares the reset grasp with Toss Ball and Wipe Window: closed fingers, with the object spawned between them and overlapping them.

What changed (shared by Peg in Hole, Wipe Window, Toss Ball)

  • FA-Hexa now sets the reset finger opening from the object's width: opening = width / 2 + 1 mm.
  • The robot config records where the palm is, and the object is seated against the palm according to its own length instead of being centered on the tool tip. Robots without a palm setting behave as before.
  • The expert and the success check are the official ones; with the peg held deeper, all 7 runs since have passed the hole plane.

The other 7 tasks (Press Button, NDT, Cabinet Pick & Place, Lemon Harvesting, Rotate Valve, Push Slider, Pull Lever) are unchanged; the official experts work on FA-Hexa as is.

Code changes are on the AM-Bench branch omnihexa-task-fixes and are not committed yet.
