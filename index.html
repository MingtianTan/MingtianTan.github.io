Mingtian Tan

Research Assistant, Intelligent Robotics and Autonomy Lab, University of Michigan

I am a research assistant in the Intelligent Robotics and Autonomy Lab (iRaL) at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, advised by Prof. Vasileios Tzoumas and working closely with Hongyu Zhou. I received my M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering (Robotics) from Michigan in 2026. In summer 2025 I was a research intern at the FAST Lab, Zhejiang University, with Prof. Fei Gao.

My research is on embodied intelligence for aerial robots: flying agents that perceive and act in the physical world. I currently work on aerial manipulation with vision-language-action models coupled to learned flight policies, and on online residual adaptation for agile flight under unknown, non-stationary disturbances.

Publications

Dual Gradient Consensus for Adaptive Learning to Transient Out-of-Distribution Disturbances in Quadrotor Control

Mingtian Tan, Vasileios Tzoumas

Under review at IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), 2027

Two gradient memories, long-term and short-term, vote on every online update of the learned disturbance model: when they disagree the step size shrinks, so a brief out-of-distribution pulse cannot corrupt the model, and once they agree again after a lasting shift the step size is restored. Lowest tracking error among all baselines on four mixed-disturbance benchmarks.

Efficient Domain-Adaptive Policy Learning via Kernel Representation with Application to Quadrotor Control under Non-Stationary Disturbances

Hongyu Zhou, Mingtian Tan, Vasileios Tzoumas

arXiv:2606.13842, 2026. Under review at IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L)

arXiv

A kernel (random Fourier feature) disturbance model trained offline through differentiable simulation and adapted online by least squares. Up to 60–70% lower tracking error than adaptive-control and RL baselines in simulation, with hardware experiments on a Crazyflie under wind, payload, and ground-effect disturbances.

A Residential Robot for Chinese Cupping Physiotherapy

Mingtian Tan, B. Gu, C. Gu, Y. Lu, M. Leach, Q. Zhang, Q. Bu

Robot Intelligence Technology and Applications 8 (RiTA 2023), Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems, vol. 1132, pp. 321–335, Springer, 2024 (oral)

A home-use cupping-therapy robot: an ABB arm with a custom suction gripper plans and executes therapeutic trajectories on the back, validated in controlled human tests. Second place, Jiangsu Provincial Intelligent Robot Innovation Competition (team lead).

Projects

Aerial manipulation with a flight-conditioned arm VLA

iRaL, University of Michigan · Oct 2026 – present

project page

A two-policy architecture for a fully actuated hexarotor with a 4-DoF arm in AM-Bench: a lightweight Flight DiT outputs flight action chunks and a flight-intent token that conditions a π0.5 arm policy. I built the demonstration dataset from scripted specialists after auditing and fixing the official task policies (44/60 → 60/60 successful runs). Evaluation against Diffusion Policy and LoRA / fully fine-tuned π0.5 across 12 tasks and 48 disturbance variants is in progress.

Pathos: a LiDAR-based agile quadrotor research platform

iRaL, University of Michigan · Sep – Dec 2025

FAST-LIO guide

Designed and built a LiDAR-equipped agile quadrotor on the Agilicious stack: 3D-printed sensor payload and mounts, Livox MID360 + FAST-LIO odometry with EKF pose fusion on a Jetson Orin, and a low-latency flight-controller link. Deployed an RFF-based simultaneous system-identification MPC on the vehicle; flight tests showed better disturbance rejection than the baselines.

Online swept-volume planning for dynamic environments

FAST Lab, Zhejiang University · May – Jul 2025

Extended the SVSDF planner from offline to online swept-volume computation, with a centroid–boundary formulation that gives exact continuous-time contact constraints on the Lie group of rigid motions. A parallel CUDA kernel brought each replan from 40 ms to 4 ms (10×), enabling real-time obstacle-aware replanning.

Autonomous aerial robot

B.Eng. final-year project, XJTLU · Oct 2023 – May 2024

video

Built the power and perception stack of a quadrotor: stereo VINS-Fusion odometry fused with flight-controller IMU data on a ROS 2 onboard computer, EGO-Planner for onboard planning and mapping, and state-machine safety logic for autonomous take-off and navigation; validated in outdoor waypoint missions.
