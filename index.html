About

I'm Mingtian Tan, a graduate researcher at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, in the Intelligent Robotics and Autonomy Lab (iRaL) with Prof. Vasileios Tzoumas and Hongyu Zhou.

My research interest lies in the combination of robotics, learning, and control: online residual adaptation for autonomous flight under unknown disturbances. I care about residual models that can be trained across domains and updated in real time, and about keeping that update reliable when the residual is only partly a signal of the current domain—when a lasting change is mixed with a short out-of-distribution transient.

My research has been mainly applied to quadrotor trajectory tracking under unknown, time-varying disturbances.

Research Interests

  • Online residual optimization under mixed disturbances
  • Domain-adaptive policy learning
  • Agile flight under unknown aerodynamics
